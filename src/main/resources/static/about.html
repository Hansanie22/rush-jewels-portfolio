Welcome to Velora Fine Jewellery

For over three decades, Velora Fine Jewellery has been Sri Lanka's premier destination for exquisite, handcrafted jewelry. Located in the heart of Colombo, we combine traditional craftsmanship with contemporary design to create pieces that tell your unique story.

Every piece in our collection is meticulously crafted by master artisans who pour their passion and expertise into creating jewelry that transcends generations. From ethically sourced gemstones to conflict-free diamonds, we are committed to excellence in every facet.

Our Journey

1995 - The Beginning

Founded by master jeweler Chaminda Perera in a small workshop in Colombo, Velora Fine Jewellery began with a vision to bring world-class jewelry to Sri Lanka.

2003 - Expansion

Opened our flagship showroom in Colombo 07, showcasing our expanded collection of bridal jewelry, gemstone pieces, and custom designs.

2010 - International Recognition

Received the Sri Lankan Gem & Jewelry Excellence Award, establishing Velora Fine Jewellery as a leader in innovative design and ethical sourcing.

2018 - Digital Transformation

Launched our online platform, bringing our exquisite collections to customers across Sri Lanka and beyond, with secure delivery and authentication.

2025 - Sustainable Future

Committed to 100% ethical sourcing and carbon-neutral operations, pioneering sustainable luxury in Sri Lanka's jewelry industry.
